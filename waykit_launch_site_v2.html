A new kind of asset

Compiled expertise is a new category.

A pack isn't just for moving your own knowledge between machines. It's a unit of compiled human expertise — evidence-anchored, portable, verifiable. The same format that lets you take your engineering standards to a new job is the format that lets an expert publish their knowledge as something others can actually use.

🧠

Expert packs

A 20-year architect publishes their distributed systems mental model. A sales trainer packages their methodology. A therapist compiles CBT techniques for therapeutic tool builders. The expertise travels separately from the person.

🏢

Team packs

An engineering org ships an onboarding pack to every new hire's AI on day one. An agency ships their creative review methodology to every client project. Compiled standards that move with the team, not the wiki.

🎓

Course packs

A professor publishes a course as a compiled pack — lecture notes, rubrics, worked examples. Students install it into their study AI. The material is bounded, cited, and verified — not a generic internet summary.

Interested in publishing a pack?The format exists. The tooling is here. If you're building expertise worth compiling — as an expert, a team, or a creator — we want to talk.
