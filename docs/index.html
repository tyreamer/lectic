The problem

Your AI forgets what you teach it.

You find a great video, send it to an AI, and have a useful conversation. Two weeks later, that knowledge is basically gone.

Lectic keeps the useful parts, connected to the material you trust. So your AI can apply what you learned to the next project—and the one after that.

Technical details and boundaries

Lectic preserves the original sources alongside derived knowledge. It can verify that quoted material matches saved text, but it cannot decide whether a source’s advice is correct—you still use your judgment.

It supports public web articles, YouTube captions, and text notes or transcripts. Other files can be retained as attachments. Read the documentation.
