Know what is ready

Traceable work.
Honest limits.

Lectic checks that quotes and stored artifacts match their sources. That does not prove the source is true or that an interpretation is correct. Your assistant still needs to reason carefully.

Text and transcripts can be processed. YouTube caption retrieval is optional and can fail. Other links are saved without downloading their pages. PDF, image, audio and video attachments are preserved; built-in OCR and transcription are not available.

Hosted chat needs a running Lectic connector. Anyone holding a share link can read and change that library. The alpha has not yet demonstrated an advantage in a completed comparative user study.
