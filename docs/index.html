Transparent boundaries

Exact quotes.
Honest limits.

Lectic checks that every quote matches the original source text. That gives you clear proof of where advice came from, but your AI still needs your expertise and judgement.

Text, notes, and transcripts (.txt, .md, .vtt, .srt) are fully processed. YouTube caption download is supported automatically. All playbooks remain 100% private on your machine in ~/.lectic.
